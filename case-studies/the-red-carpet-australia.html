Case Study

Generated 82% Growth in Organic Traffic for The Red Carpet Australia

By partnering with Online Marketing Champ, the client transformed its SEO strategy and achieved market-leading visibility.

Industry:

Home Decor & Interior Furnishings

Services:

About The Red Carpet Australia

The Red Carpet Australia is a premium destination for authentic Persian rugs and carpets in Melbourne, offering a carefully curated range that blends heritage craftsmanship with modern interior aesthetics. From luxury handmade statement pieces to discounted Iranian rugs, the brand provides options for every home, style, and budget.

Their collection spans classical Persian, transitional, tribal, and contemporary designs, positioning fine handmade rugs as timeless décor elements and long-term investment pieces.

The Challenge

The Red Carpet Australia struggled to translate its strong offline reputation into online visibility, especially within the highly competitive Melbourne market.

The primary challenge was limited organic presence for high-intent local searches. Key buyer-driven queries, such as Authentic Persian Rugs in Melbourne, were not ranking on the first page of Google.

  • Lack of Melbourne-focused keyword targeting across product and category pages.
  • Insufficient local SEO signals to strengthen geographic relevance.
  • Technical SEO gaps affecting crawlability and search performance.
  • Metadata not optimised to improve click-through rates.

How Did We Achieve the Results?

We implemented a focused SEO strategy aligned with local buyer intent and premium brand positioning. By combining technical improvements, targeted content optimisation, and strong local SEO execution, we helped the brand gain visibility.

Optimise Product & Category Pages

We optimised product and category pages to match high-intent Melbourne-based search queries. This improved relevance, improved click-through rates, and drove more qualified organic traffic.

Technical SEO & Website Performance

A comprehensive technical SEO audit helped us resolve crawlability issues. We improved site structure, optimised page speed, and implemented structured data to ensure stronger search engine trust signals.

Local SEO & Authority Building

To strengthen local search presence, we optimised the Google Business Profile, implemented location-focused content, and built authority, which boosted local visibility and engagement.
